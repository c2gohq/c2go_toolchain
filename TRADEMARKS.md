project branding. They are not included in the software license grants merely
because their files appear in this repository. No statement here claims that a

to state compatibility, or to reproduce unmodified identification required in
an authorized redistribution. You may not use the names or logo in a way that
suggests sponsorship, endorsement, official status, or origin when none exists.

Modified forks should use a distinguishable name and branding and should state
clearly that they are unofficial. Permission is required for merchandise,
commercial branding, domain names, product names, or prominent logo use beyond
truthful referential use.

The current logo is a provisional pre-release asset. Before trademark filing
or a major commercial launch, the project owner should obtain a professional
similarity and trademark clearance review.
