> **This document is not a commercial license, offer, price list, or grant of
> rights.**

The selected structure uses an authorized individual as licensor for original
C2Go material that individual owns or is otherwise entitled to license. Every
commercial license must be separately executed and identify the parties by
their full legal names, the covered repositories, tags and commit hashes, the
licensed rights, territory, term, products/deployments, fees, taxes, support,
termination, and third-party exclusions.

GitHub Sponsors may be designated as the payment channel if the individual is
eligible to receive payouts. The agreement, not the sponsorship action, grants
rights. A tier selection, payment, receipt, invoice, export, or transaction
metadata alone grants no software or trademark rights and must not be described
as a no-benefit donation when it is consideration for a license.

This repository cannot commercially relicense component or generated material
owned by LLVM, musl, Go, PureGo, Apple/FreeBSD/XNU/MinGW contributors, users, or
other third parties. Their original licenses and notices remain effective.

Before offering commercial terms, the project must verify the individual's
legal identity and chain of title, adopt an appropriate contributor agreement,
complete component provenance, publish an authorized contact, and have
qualified counsel approve the agreement and payment/tax process.
