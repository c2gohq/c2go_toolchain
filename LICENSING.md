## Component boundaries

This repository aggregates independently licensed components through Git
submodules. Aggregation and version pinning do not replace or narrow their
licenses:

- c2go-clang remains under `Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception` and its existing
  per-file/third-party notices;
- original c2go-bind material is intended under `AGPL-3.0-only` or a separate
  commercial agreement, while PureGo/Go-derived and other third-party portions
  retain their terms;
- c2go-libc is a mixed work containing original C2Go, musl-derived, and other
  third-party material under their respective terms; and
- the musl fork retains musl's permissive terms and file-specific notices.

A commercial C2Go agreement can license only rights controlled by its named
licensor. It cannot relicense LLVM, musl, Go, PureGo, Apple/FreeBSD/XNU/MinGW
material, user source code, or other third-party material.

## Generated output

Compiler or generator use does not by itself transfer ownership of user input.
Generated packages can nevertheless contain copied C2Go support code and
third-party-derived support templates. Those retained portions keep their
applicable licenses and notices. Release bundles must describe the actual mix
rather than labeling every generated byte as wholly C2Go-owned or wholly AGPL.
